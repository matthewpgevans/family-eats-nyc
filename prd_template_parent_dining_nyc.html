Parent-friendly NYC dining directory  ·  Fill this out, then paste into Claude Code

1
Overview

One paragraph — what the product is and why it exists.

Product name
e.g. "TableTot", "Family Eats NYC"…
One-line description
Help parents in NYC find restaurants where dining with kids is actually enjoyable.
Problem being solved
Parents waste time searching Yelp/Google only to arrive and find no high chairs, loud adults-only vibes, or 45-min waits. There's no trusted source that filters by family-specific signals.
2
Target users

Who are you building for? Be specific — age of kids matters.

Primary user
NYC parents with kids 0–8 years old. Busy, time-poor, skew millennial. Rely on their phone for decisions.
Secondary user
Grandparents visiting, nannies, or out-of-town family planning a NYC meal with kids in tow.
User pain points
No high chairs · Loud/unsuitable atmosphere · Unhealthy or no kids menu · No stroller access · Can't filter by neighborhood
